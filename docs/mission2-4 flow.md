### Quest 1- Investigation: Observing/Noticing

This would invlove what entire observation breakdown that we did now with different sources. Observation itself will answer the following questions, which users will fill out as an when they find out more about every observation :

- Who exactly has this problem? Can I name 10 specific people? (second part maybe not be suited here)

- How do they currently deal with it? *(If the answer is "nothing," that's often a red flag — it means it's not urgent enough to act on.)*

- What does it cost them — time, money, stress — if it goes unsolved?

- How often does it happen? Daily? Monthly? Once a year?

- What have they already tried? Why did they stop?

- Is this getting worse or better over time?

All this will be saved inside user_opportunities which will be both in database and hydrated to client stores

### Quest 2 - Qualification 2

Can I do anything about it? **Key questions for every opportunity**

- What do I already know about this domain?

- What would I need to learn, and how long would that realistically take?

- Who would I need access to — customers, suppliers, experts, partners?

- What are the hard parts I can't yet see?

- Is there a version of this I could deliver in weeks, not years?

- Who has solved something adjacent that I could learn from?

## Quest 2 Action

Filter out the ones which fail "Can I do anything about it?"



### Quest 3 - Qualification 3

**Is This Worth Solving as a Business?**

**Key questions:**

- Who pays? Is the payer the same as the person with the problem?

- How much would they pay? What do they currently spend on alternatives — money, time, or workarounds?

- Is this a painkiller or a vitamin? *(Painkillers get bought immediately. Vitamins get postponed forever.)*

- How many of these people exist, and can I reach them?

- Who else is solving this? What do they do well, and where are they weak?

- What would need to be true for this to be a business, not a hobby?

### Quest 3 Action - Shortlist

Pick top 3 after comparing the opportunities which users still have after filtering out ones in quest2

### Mission Action

Pick one from shortlisted opoprtunities to pressure Test in mission 3



## Mission 3 - Pressure Test

**Will They Actually Act?**

**Key questions:**

- What's the smallest thing I can offer that they'd say yes to?

- What counts as a real "yes"? *(Not "I'd use that" — a deposit, a booking, a commitment.)*

- What did people actually do, versus what they said?

- Who said no, and why? *(No's are more informative than yeses.)*

- What surprised me — something I expected and didn't get, or something I didn't expect and did?

- If I asked for money today, what would happen?
